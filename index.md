# Privacy Policy for BigPurplePhone

**Last updated:** 2026-10-08

The BigPurplePhone app is provided by Henderson and Associates Pty Ltd, trading as BigPurplePhone. The app is free to download; subscription plans may apply.

BigPurplePhone is a simplified caregiving phone for elderly users. It serves as the device's default phone dialer and SMS handler and provides SOS emergency assistance, caregiver location sharing, simplified calling and messaging, medication reminders, and video calls.

## Information Collection and Use

BigPurplePhone stores some information on your device and transmits some information to our servers (api.bigpurplephone.com) so that authorized caregivers can support the user. The information we handle is limited to what the app's features require:

- **Account and profile information.** When the device is registered with the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform, the user's name, phone number, profile photo, device identifier, and push-notification token are transmitted to and stored on the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform.

- **Location.** The app collects device location (foreground and, during a caregiver-initiated "find my phone" session, background) and reports it to the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform so the user's authorized caregiver can locate the device. Location is shared only with the user's authorized caregiver account.
- **Weather.** The weather feature sends the device's coordinates (latitude and longitude) to the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform backend to display the local weather forecast.
- **Contacts.** Contacts are read to build the simplified calling and messaging lists. Care contacts are synced with the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform.
- **Phone and call log.** The app is the device's default dialer: it places and answers calls and displays the call history. Call log entries are stored on the device.
- **SOS / emergency assistance.** When the emergency button is triggered, the app sends the user's account identifier and the device's current location to the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform, which notifies the user's emergency contacts and authorized caregivers.
- **SMS and MMS.** The app is the device's default SMS handler: it sends, receives, and displays messages. Messages are stored on the device only. MMS messages are sent and received through your mobile carrier.
- **Camera and photos.** The camera is used for profile photos and photo sharing. Photos you take or share in the app are uploaded to our servers and made visible to the user's authorized caregivers.
- **Microphone and audio.** The microphone is used for phone calls and video calls. The app does not record audio in the background.
- **Device identifiers.** The app uses a device identifier (Android ID, or a random identifier generated by the app) to register the device with the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform and deliver push notifications to the correct device.
- **Reminders.** Medication and other reminders are synced with the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform (authorized caregivers can schedule them, and the device reports reminder status back) and stored on your device.
- **Vaccination certificate.** The app can display a vaccination certificate and COVID check-in code issued through the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform; this information is delivered from the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform to the device for display.
- **Installed apps.** The home-screen app menu is built from the apps installed on the device; this list is processed entirely on-device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party.

## Third-Party Services and Infrastructure

The app uses the following third-party services and infrastructure:

- **Firebase Analytics** (Google) — app usage statistics: [https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)
- **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google) — crash reporting: [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google) — push notifications (e.g. incoming calls, SOS alerts): [https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google Play Services** (Google) — location services: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Video calling (self-hosted).** Video calls are relayed through BigPurplePhone's own video server (Jitsi-based, self-hosted at jitsi.bigpurplephone.com). Audio and video are processed to connect your call and are not routed through a third-party conferencing service.

Each third-party service processes data under its own privacy policy.

## Diagnostics and Log Data

Firebase Analytics collects app usage events and device information (device model, operating system version, app version). Firebase Crashlytics collects crash reports, including stack traces, device model, and operating system version, to help us diagnose and fix errors. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a push token to deliver notifications to your device.

## Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. Data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using TLS. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## Cross-Border Disclosure

Some information may be stored or processed in Australia or overseas — including on Google/Firebase infrastructure in the United States and by other service providers. Where personal information is disclosed to overseas recipients, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is handled in accordance with this policy.

## Access, Correction and Complaints

You (or your authorized caregiver) may request access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. We respond within a reasonable period, typically within 30 days.

If you have a complaint about how we handle personal information, contact us first; we will investigate and respond, typically within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at https://www.oaic.gov.au.

## Data Retention and Deletion

The app does not create user accounts in the app itself: the device is registered with the BigPurplePhone Carer Platform as part of setting up the service. We retain personal information for as long as the device's registration is active, and we delete or de-identify it once it is no longer required for the Service. You (or your authorized caregiver) may ask us to delete photos, location history, the device's registration, or other personal information at any time by contacting us.

## Sensitive Information — Health and Safety Data

Some information the app handles is health information (medication reminders, vaccination certificates) or safety-related information (SOS alerts, location sharing). This information is collected only with consent — given by the user or, where the user is unable to consent, by their authorized caregiver — is shared only with the user's authorized caregivers, and is handled with the additional protection required for sensitive information under Australian privacy law.

## Consent by Authorized Caregivers

BigPurplePhone is designed for elderly users, some of whom may not be able to manage privacy decisions themselves. Where the user is unable to give consent, an authorized caregiver may give consent on their behalf, and may exercise the user's privacy rights (access, correction, deletion, complaints) and manage the user's privacy settings.

## Children's Privacy

This Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, or if you wish to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or lodge a complaint, do not hesitate to contact us at: hello@bigpurplephone.com.au. We aim to respond within 30 days.
